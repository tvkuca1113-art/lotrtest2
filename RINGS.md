# Prstenovi

Podaci ispod odgovaraju sadržajnim definicijama igre. Brojčana snaga raste za 6% po rangu, do ×1.54 na rangu 10. Rasponi, broj meta i kontrola mase imaju zasebne granice. Najviši rang je min(10, 1 + floor((level − 1) / 3)).

## Ember — etapa 1

- Aktivno (8s osnovnog oporavka): A 100° fire arc deals 30 damage and burns for 12 more over 3 seconds. Ignites marked brambles.
- Potpora: Weapon hits add 2 burn damage per second for 2 seconds. Refreshes; does not stack.
- Kuća: A warm brazier blooms. Winter enemy damage is reduced by 3%.
- Rang 4: Arc widens to 120°. Burn lasts 4 seconds.
- Rang 7: Leaves a 2-second ember patch; one pulse per target, 12 damage.
- Priča: The ring-smith’s apprentice kept one coal when all the furnaces went dark.

## Stoneward — etapa 2

- Aktivno (14s osnovnog oporavka): A barrier absorbs 40 damage for 5 seconds. Breaking or expiring bursts for 18 nearby damage.
- Potpora: Take 5% less direct damage.
- Kuća: A stone ward surrounds the chest. Start defence waves with a 20-point barrier.
- Rang 4: Barrier rises by an additional 10 points.
- Rang 7: The burst briefly staggers ordinary enemies for 0.7 seconds.
- Priča: A ring cut from a threshold that never yielded to winter.

## Windstep — etapa 4

- Aktivno (10s osnovnog oporavka): Dash 170 units in your aim direction with 0.18 seconds of safety; a cutting gust deals 28 damage.
- Potpora: Movement speed increases by 5%.
- Kuća: Banners move in the wind. Optional cache positions appear on the mission map.
- Rang 4: Dash reaches 195 units, respecting walls.
- Rang 7: Gust hits up to 5 targets instead of 3.
- Priča: A scout tied a promise to the west wind and never walked alone again.

## Thornwake — etapa 6

- Aktivno (13s osnovnog oporavka): Place a root patch. Deals 18 damage and roots ordinary enemies for 2 seconds; bosses slow by 15%.
- Potpora: Regenerate 2 extra stamina per second.
- Kuća: The garden flourishes. First-clear expeditions grant 3 extra wood, capped at 3.
- Rang 4: Patch radius increases from 85 to 100.
- Rang 7: Root duration becomes 2.5 seconds; bosses remain capped at 15% slow.
- Priča: Even the oldest thorn remembers being a flower.

## Dawnward — etapa 8

- Aktivno (12s osnovnog oporavka): A light pulse deals 32 damage and breaks visible optional dark wards. Reveals authored inscriptions.
- Potpora: Flasks recover 8 additional HP.
- Kuća: Lanterns brighten. Clues identify the closest optional secret.
- Rang 4: Pulse radius increases by 15 units.
- Rang 7: Disrupts an ordinary enemy’s telegraph once per cast.
- Priča: Its maker never saw the sun, but trusted that it would return.

## Venomcoil — etapa 9

- Aktivno (9s osnovnog oporavka): Fire a venom dart for 20 damage. Poison deals 4 per second for 4 seconds, capped at 3 stacks.
- Potpora: Weapon strikes apply a 5% slow for 1 second.
- Kuća: Glass covers the garden. Flasks recover 5 additional HP.
- Rang 4: Poison lasts 5 seconds; still capped at 3 stacks.
- Rang 7: Dart pierces one additional enemy, respecting walls.
- Priča: A gardener’s answer to the things that ate the roots.

## Frostwake — etapa 11

- Aktivno (11s osnovnog oporavka): A frost cone deals 30 damage and slows by 30% for 3 seconds. Freezes marked optional water.
- Potpora: Dodge costs 3 less stamina.
- Kuća: Frost lanterns shine. Winter movement gains 3%.
- Rang 4: Cone widens from 85° to 105°.
- Rang 7: Ordinary foes freeze for 0.7 seconds; bosses only slow.
- Priča: Water held still to let a stranger cross.

## Iron Oath — etapa 13

- Aktivno (12s osnovnog oporavka): Brace for 1.3 seconds, reducing damage by 70%. A hit within 0.45 seconds retaliates for 38 and staggers.
- Potpora: Guard reduces a further 8% damage; capped at 85% total.
- Kuća: Iron banners strengthen barricades. Defence damage taken falls by 5%.
- Rang 4: The perfect defence window increases to 0.55 seconds.
- Rang 7: Retaliation reaches 3 enemies within 130 units.
- Priča: An oath is a thing held when the hand is empty.

## Echo — etapa 14

- Aktivno (10s osnovnog oporavka): Your next weapon attack within 5 seconds repeats once at 45% damage. No ring or synergy recursion.
- Potpora: Weapon recovery is 5% faster.
- Kuća: An animated practice dummy measures your last hit and peak damage.
- Rang 4: Repeat damage increases to 50%.
- Rang 7: Repeat damage increases to 55%, the hard cap.
- Priča: A hammer’s last note, kept by a hall that missed its maker.

## Stormcall — etapa 17

- Aktivno (13s osnovnog oporavka): Lightning jumps to 3 visible foes within 250 units for 34 damage each. Wet targets take 10% more.
- Potpora: Ring cooldowns are reduced by 5%.
- Kuća: The watchtower catches quiet lightning. Show all optional cache positions.
- Rang 4: Chain cap becomes 4 targets.
- Rang 7: Chain cap becomes 5 targets; jumps never trigger further chains.
- Priča: The first storm after the siege rang like bells of relief.

## Duskveil — etapa 21

- Aktivno (11s osnovnog oporavka): Blink up to 160 units along a clear path. A shadow distracts ordinary enemies for 2 seconds and strikes once for 20.
- Potpora: Dodge cooldown is 8% shorter.
- Kuća: A shadow garden reveals the season’s alternate-route clue.
- Rang 4: Shadow lasts 2.5 seconds.
- Rang 7: Shadow’s one strike gains a 0.5-second ordinary-enemy stagger.
- Priča: A ring for those who crossed a dark room to open its window.

## Last Hearth — etapa 25

- Aktivno (16s osnovnog oporavka): Create a 4-second shelter circle. Intercept up to 5 enemy projectiles and recover 20 HP once.
- Potpora: Maximum health increases by 6%.
- Kuća: A luminous central beacon illuminates the roofs and your six trophies.
- Rang 4: Shelter intercepts 7 projectiles.
- Rang 7: Shelter lasts 5 seconds and intercepts at most 9 projectiles.
- Priča: Not a ring to rule. A ring to leave a light on.

## Kombinacije

Oba prstena moraju biti u dva aktivna mjesta. Potporna i kućna mjesta ne uključuju kombinacije. Sekundarni učinci ne pokreću nove kombinacije, Echo ili beskonačne lance.

- **Wildfire wind**: Cast Windstep while a foe burns: spread a 12-damage ember to up to 3 nearby foes. 8-second internal cooldown.
- **Winter thunder**: Stormcall against a slowed foe adds one 16-damage shatter pulse to at most 3 foes. 10-second internal cooldown.
- **Bitter roots**: Thornwake poisons rooted foes for one stack. Maximum 4 targets; 10-second internal cooldown.
- **The held promise**: A successful Iron Oath counter with Stoneward grants a 20-point barrier and a 15-damage retaliation. 12-second internal cooldown.
- **After the shadow**: Duskveil while Echo is ready makes one delayed 18-damage shadow strike on up to 3 foes. 10-second internal cooldown.
- **A light for everyone**: Dawnward within shelter heals 10 HP and enlarges its radius by 20, once per shelter. 14-second internal cooldown.
